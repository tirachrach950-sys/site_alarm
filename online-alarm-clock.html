Frequently asked questions

Does the online alarm work if I close the tab?

No. A website alarm only rings while this page stays open in your browser. Keep the tab open, keep the volume up, and plug in your device. For important mornings, also set an alarm on your phone.

Will the alarm work on my phone?

On many phones it works if the screen stays on and the page stays in the foreground. Because browsers pause pages in the background, use your phone's own alarm app as the main alarm.

Why is there no sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page. Press Start alarm, check that your volume is up, and press Test sound to confirm.
