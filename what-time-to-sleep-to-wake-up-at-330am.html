What time should I go to bed to wake up at 3:30 AM?

To wake up at 3:30 AM, go to bed at 6:15 PM, 7:45 PM or 9:15 PM. The best choice for most people is 7:45 PM.

9:15 PM6h sleep · 4 cycles
10:45 PM4h 30m sleep · 3 cycles

Frequently asked questions

What time should I go to bed to wake up at 3:30 AM?

To wake up at 3:30 AM, go to bed at 6:15 PM (9 hours), 7:45 PM (7.5 hours) or 9:15 PM (6 hours). 7:45 PM works for most adults.

Is 7:45 PM a good bedtime for a 3:30 AM alarm?

Yes. Going to bed at 7:45 PM gives you 7.5 hours of sleep after about 15 minutes to fall asleep, ending at the end of a sleep cycle.

What if I can't go to bed that early?

Pick the next bedtime on the list. Four cycles (6 hours) is the minimum for a short night, and you should make it up with an earlier bedtime the next day.
