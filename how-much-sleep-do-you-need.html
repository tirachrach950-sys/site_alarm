How Much Sleep Do You Need?

Needs change with age. These are the ranges commonly recommended by sleep experts.

AgeRecommended sleep
Newborn (0–3 months)14–17 hours
Infant (4–11 months)12–15 hours
Toddler (1–2 years)11–14 hours
Preschool (3–5 years)10–13 hours
School age (6–13 years)9–11 hours
Teenager (14–17 years)8–10 hours
Adult (18–64 years)7–9 hours
Older adult (65+)7–8 hours

For adults, 7 to 9 hours means 5 or 6 sleep cycles. Use the sleep calculator to find the bedtime or wake-up time that fits.

These are general guidelines. If you are tired all day even after enough sleep, talk to a doctor.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get by on 5 hours of sleep?

A few people may, but most adults need 7 or more hours. Regular short sleep is linked with lower focus and poorer health.

Does the quality of sleep matter as much as the hours?

Yes. Regular times, a dark and cool room, and less screen time before bed all improve the quality of your sleep.
