What time should I wake up if I go to bed at 11:00 PM?

If you fall asleep at about 11:15 PM, set your alarm for 8:15 AM, 6:45 AM or 5:15 AM. The best choice for most people is 6:45 AM.

11:00 PM is a classic bedtime. This is the most common bedtime for adults, and it fits a normal work or school morning. The table counts 15 minutes to fall asleep, then adds full 90-minute cycles, so each wake-up time is the end of a cycle.

Tips for waking up at 6:45 AM

  • Keep your bedroom cool, dark and quiet.
  • Put your phone away 30 to 60 minutes before 11:00 PM.
  • Avoid caffeine in the 6 hours before bed.
  • Get daylight soon after you wake up, to set your body clock.

Frequently asked questions

What time should I wake up if I go to bed at 11:00 PM?

If you go to bed at 11:00 PM and fall asleep in about 15 minutes, the best times to wake up are 8:15 AM, 6:45 AM or 5:15 AM. 6:45 AM gives you 7.5 hours of sleep, which suits most adults.

Is 6:45 AM a good wake-up time if I sleep at 11:00 PM?

Yes. 6:45 AM lands at the end of your fifth sleep cycle, after 7.5 hours of sleep. That is the amount most adults need, and it makes waking easier.

What if I fall asleep faster or slower?

Move the times by the difference. If you fall asleep in 5 minutes instead of 15, wake 10 minutes earlier. If it takes 30 minutes, wake 15 minutes later.
