How the sleep calculator works

Sleep moves in cycles of about 90 minutes. Each cycle passes through light sleep, deep sleep and REM sleep. If your alarm rings during deep sleep you feel groggy, even after enough hours. If it rings at the end of a cycle you wake more easily. The calculator adds 15 minutes for falling asleep, then counts 90-minute cycles.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to fall asleep?

Most healthy adults need about 10 to 20 minutes. This calculator uses 15 minutes. If you usually fall asleep faster or slower, shift the times by the difference.

What is a sleep cycle?

A sleep cycle lasts about 90 minutes and moves through light sleep, deep sleep and REM sleep. Waking at the end of a cycle usually feels easier than waking in the middle of deep sleep.

How many hours of sleep do adults need?

Most adults do best with 7 to 9 hours. That is 5 or 6 full cycles. Needs vary from person to person.

Is this sleep calculator accurate?

It is a planning tool based on the average 90-minute sleep cycle and 15 minutes to fall asleep. Real cycles vary from 70 to 120 minutes, so use the results as a guide and adjust after a few days.

What time should I go to bed if I wake up at 6 AM?

For 6 AM, try 9:15 PM, 10:45 PM or 12:15 AM. Those give you 6, 5 or 4 full sleep cycles after you fall asleep. 10:45 PM (7.5 hours of sleep) works for most adults.
